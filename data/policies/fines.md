# Fines and fees

## Overdue fines

Overdue books are charged €0.25 per day per item, starting the day after the due date.
The fine for a single item stops growing once it reaches €10.
Renewing an overdue book does not cancel the fine already owed: it stays on the loan.

## Blocked accounts

When your unpaid fines reach €10 or more, your account is blocked.
A blocked account cannot renew loans or place new holds. You can still return books.
Pay fines at any branch desk or online through the student portal; the block is lifted immediately.

## Lost or damaged items

A book that is 60 days overdue is treated as lost. You are charged the replacement cost of the book plus a
€15 processing fee. If you later return the book in good condition, the replacement cost is refunded.
