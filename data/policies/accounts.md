# Library accounts

## Who can borrow

Enrolled students and university staff can borrow books with their member ID. Visitors can read in the
library but cannot borrow.

## Identity verification

Before the library assistant shows or changes anything in your account, it asks for your member ID and your
last name. Library staff never share account information with anyone other than the account holder.

## Privacy

Your loan history is deleted 30 days after a book is returned, in line with GDPR data minimisation.
The library assistant does not store your conversations after the session ends.

## Updating contact details

You can change the email address the library uses for notices in your online account. Notices about
holds ready for pickup and upcoming due dates are sent to that address.
