# Loans and renewals

## Loan periods

Students can borrow books for 21 days. Staff can borrow books for 42 days.
The due date is printed on your receipt and shown in your online account.

## Renewals

You can renew a loan online, at the desk, or through the library assistant.
Each renewal extends the current due date by one loan period: 21 days for students and 42 days for staff.
A loan can be renewed at most twice: a maximum of 2 renewals per loan. After that the book must be returned.

A renewal is refused when:

- another member has placed a hold on the book and is waiting for it;
- the loan is more than 14 days overdue;
- your account is blocked because of unpaid fines.

## Returning books

Return books at any branch desk or in the 24-hour book drop outside the main entrance.
Returns made through the book drop are checked in the next working day, and no fine is charged for that day.
