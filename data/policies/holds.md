# Holds and reservations

## Placing a hold

You can place a hold on any book in the catalogue, whether or not a copy is on the shelf.
If a copy is available, it is set aside for you straight away and the hold becomes ready for pickup.
If every copy is on loan, you join the waiting queue for that title.

You can have at most 5 active holds at a time.
You cannot place a hold on a book you currently have on loan, and you cannot hold the same title twice.

## Pickup window

When a hold is ready, you have 5 days to collect the book from the holds shelf at the main desk.
After that the hold expires and the copy goes to the next member in the queue.

## Hold queue

Holds are served first come, first served. Your position in the queue is shown in your account.
A member who is waiting for a book stops other borrowers from renewing it.

## Cancelling a hold

You can cancel a hold at any time. Cancelling a hold that was ready for pickup releases the copy to the
next member in the queue, or back to the shelf if nobody is waiting.
