# Library services

## Opening hours

The main library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 22:00 and on Saturday from 9:00 to 18:00.
It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Opening hours are reduced during university holidays.

## Study rooms

Group study rooms can be booked through the library website for up to 2 hours per day. The library
assistant cannot book study rooms.

## Printing and scanning

Printers and scanners are on the ground floor. Printing costs €0.05 per black-and-white page and is paid
with your student card. Scanning to email is free.

## Wi-Fi

The eduroam network is available everywhere in the building. Sign in with your university account.
